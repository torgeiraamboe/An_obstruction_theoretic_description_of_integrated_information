
\begin{abstract}
\textbf{%
Given a physical system, we assign to it a presheaf of local factorization data, whose \v{C}ech cohomology measures the failure of local causal factorizations to glue into a compatible global factorization. The first degree obstruction classes for this cohomology theory provide a functorial notion of irreducibility, as suggested by Tull--Kleiner. We prove that vanishing integrated information implies the vanishing of the first degree obstruction classes, showing that they detect the same notion of reducibility as classical Integrated Information Theory. The higher degree obstruction classes naturally define a concept of higher order integration, giving a full hierarchy in a unified framework.}
\end{abstract}
